About the CAPTCHA Speed Test

Who built this, why, and how the site makes money

← Back to the game

I'm YOUR NAME, and I built this test because every explanation of "get paid to solve CAPTCHAs" skips the one thing you can measure in a minute: how fast and how accurately you can actually read warped text. So the game measures exactly that, then turns your result into a realistic income estimate instead of a hype number.

What is on the site

A 60-second text CAPTCHA test with codes that get more distorted as you improve, a boss round with Google's real reCAPTCHA v2 checkbox, and a scorecard with your WPM, accuracy and estimated earnings. There are no accounts, nothing to install, and the game runs entirely in your browser.

Why the earnings estimate is low

I would rather you be pleasantly surprised than misled. The estimate assumes about /bin/sh.50 to .00 per 1,000 solved text CAPTCHAs, uses your accuracy as a multiplier, and takes 30% off your test pace for loading time and idle gaps. Real rates depend on the service, the task type and demand, so check them yourself before relying on any figure.

How this site makes money

  • Advertising. Display ads (Google AdSense, once approved) appear above and below the game.
  • Affiliate link. The button on your scorecard and the link in the article on the game page are referral links to 2Captcha. If you sign up through them I may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. I am not employed by or officially partnered with 2Captcha.

The estimate and scoring rules are the same whether or not you click anything.

Spotted a bug or have a suggestion?

Tell me on the contact page. Reports of codes that were hard to read for the wrong reasons, mobile keyboard issues, and accessibility problems are the most useful.
