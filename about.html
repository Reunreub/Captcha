About the CAPTCHA Practice

Who built this, why, and how the site makes money

← Back to the practice

I'm Ren. I built this site as a simple practice tool for people who want to see how quickly and accurately they can solve CAPTCHA-style challenges before spending time on paid CAPTCHA work.

What is on the site

The main practice is a 60-second text CAPTCHA test. Other modes add math, color and grid-style challenges. The Boss Round uses Google reCAPTCHA v2. Your practice results are calculated in your browser.

Why the earnings estimate is low

From my experience, I make about $0.50 a day without the booster. With the 100% booster available on 2Captcha, I make about $1.70 a day. That is my personal experience, not a guaranteed rate, and earnings can vary by region, task availability, CAPTCHA type and how much time you work.
