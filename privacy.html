What the game itself stores

There are no accounts and no sign-up. Your answers, solve counts, WPM and accuracy are calculated in your browser and are never sent to me. The only thing saved is your best WPM, kept in your browser's local storage under the key captchaBestWpm so the game can show "Your best". It stays on your device, and you can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.

Google reCAPTCHA (boss round)

The boss round loads Google reCAPTCHA v2 from google.com. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and application data, along with your IP address and browser signals, and sends it to Google for analysis. Its use is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The script loads when the page opens; if you block it, the game still works and the boss round can be skipped.
