What CAPTCHA-solving work actually pays

2Captcha currently advertises CAPTCHA-solving work as a way to earn online. Its worker page says the amount depends on service load and the type of CAPTCHA. It currently advertises $0.50 for 1–2 hours depending on service load, and says the minimum payout starts at $0.50. It also lists several withdrawal methods with different minimums and says there are no payout fees for the listed methods.

My take: Yes, CAPTCHA solving can make money, but it depends heavily on how much time you work, how many tasks are available, and your solving speed. I would treat it as side income rather than a guaranteed hourly wage.

$0.50 minimum

That is the minimum 2Captcha currently advertises for some payout methods—not a promise that you will earn $0.50 in a particular amount of time.

How fast can you get paid?

2Captcha's current worker information lists minimum withdrawal amounts by wallet. For example, it currently lists $0.50 for WebMoney, $1.00 for AirTM, and $0.25 for Bitcoin Cash, with other methods having different minimums. Wallet availability can depend on your country.

2Captcha says the listed payout methods have no payout fees. Processing speed can depend on the method and current service conditions, so check the withdrawal screen before relying on a specific timing.

What this website does

This website is practice only. It does not submit your answers to 2Captcha, does not create paid tasks for you, and does not pay you for completing the practice challenges. The point is to let you test your speed and accuracy before deciding whether CAPTCHA-solving work is worth your time.

What about reCAPTCHA?

2Captcha's worker information describes work involving different CAPTCHA types. This site's Boss Round uses Google's reCAPTCHA v2 widget as a practice challenge. It is separate from 2Captcha's paid worker system.
