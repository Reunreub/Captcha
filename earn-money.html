What CAPTCHA-solving work actually pays

Can CAPTCHA solving really pay?

Some CAPTCHA-solving platforms pay workers, but rates and task availability vary. Treat it as small side income rather than a salary.

$1 / 1,000

2Captcha currently lists different rates by CAPTCHA type. Its reCAPTCHA v2 software-solving rate has been listed at $1 per 1,000 solved, while other CAPTCHA rates vary.

Your location matters

Rates, available tasks, payout methods and demand can change. Check the current figures on the platform before relying on an estimate.

Use this site as practice

The speed test is a training tool. It does not send your solves to 2Captcha and it does not itself pay you.
