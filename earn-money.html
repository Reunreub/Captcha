What CAPTCHA-solving work actually pays

From my experience, I make about $0.50 a day without the booster. With the 100% booster available on 2Captcha, I make about $1.70 a day. Your results can be different depending on your region, available tasks, CAPTCHA type, solving speed and how much time you work.

2Captcha says reCAPTCHA v2 can pay around $0.00100 per CAPTCHA in its worker system. With a 100% boost, that becomes $0.00200 per CAPTCHA. For example, 100 reCAPTCHA v2 tasks at $0.00200 each would be $0.20. It takes real work and patience to build up small amounts.

For other CAPTCHA types, the rates I commonly see are roughly $0.00095–$0.00100 for image CAPTCHAs and around $0.00020 for text CAPTCHAs. Rates can change with demand and task availability.

My experience: CAPTCHA solving can make money, but it is slow work and depends heavily on how much time you put in. If you are struggling to find simple online work, 2Captcha is one option I use for side income.
